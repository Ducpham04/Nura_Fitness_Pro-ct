# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Fitness enthusiasts aged 18-40 who want AI-powered workout tracking and personalized challenges. Users are tech-savvy, value gamification, and want professional-grade fitness insights without gym equipment. Primary context: mobile and desktop workout sessions, checking progress dashboards, competing on leaderboards.

## Product Purpose

FIT Challenge is an AI-powered fitness platform that uses computer vision (MediaPipe) to track exercises, analyze form, and generate personalized training plans. Success looks like: users completing daily workouts with AI feedback, measurable body metric improvements, and active participation in challenges.

## Brand Personality

**Three words:** Electric, Precise, Empowering

**Voice:** Technical but accessible. Confident without arrogance. Motivational without being cheesy.

**Emotional goals:** Users should feel like they have a professional AI coach in their pocket. High-tech but human-centered.

## Anti-references

- Avoid generic fitness app clichés (boring blue/white, overly cheerful illustrations)
- No stock photo aesthetics
- No cluttered dashboard with 20 metrics at once
- Avoid brutalist/minimalist extremes - this is premium tech, not indie experimental

## Design Principles

1. **Performance-first aesthetics** - The UI should feel as fast and responsive as a well-executed workout
2. **Data visualization as motivation** - Charts and metrics should inspire action, not just inform
3. **Progressive disclosure** - Surface what's needed now; details available on demand
4. **Gamification without gimmicks** - Meaningful rewards and clear progress paths
5. **AI transparency** - Make the AI's analysis and recommendations understandable

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA minimum
- High contrast essential for outdoor workout visibility
- Motion reduction support for users sensitive to animations
- Touch targets minimum 44px on mobile
